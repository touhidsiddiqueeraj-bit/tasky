Welcome to Tasky

A three-column board — To Do · Working On · Done — built for speed. No sign-up needed, your data lives on your device. Every action has a keyboard shortcut.

⌨️ Quick start
Press any key to open the task input. Type and hit Enter to save.
⌨️

Adding Tasks

Press any letter or number to open the floating input. Type your task and hit Enter to save. Press Esc to cancel. Tasks always land in To Do first.

🎙️ Voice input
Hold Space to dictate — release to save. Works in Chrome, Edge & Brave.
🎯

Move & Prioritize

Click a task to select it, then press ← → to move between columns. You can also drag and drop. Press 1 2 3 for High / Medium / Low priority.

💡 Pro tip
On mobile, swipe tasks left/right to move them. Click the priority badge to cycle levels.
📋

Workspaces

Keep different projects separate with Workspaces. Switch between them using the pill bar at the top. Each workspace has its own tasks, settings, and optional collaboration.

➕ New workspace
Click ＋ New Workspace in the top bar to get started. The first workspace is always "Personal".
⊞

Task Groups

Create reusable templates from Tasky ▼ → Task Groups. Add subtasks with priorities and choose To Do or Working On.

⚡ Instant expand
Start typing a group name — matching suggestions appear. Press Enter to expand the whole group onto the board instantly.
☁️

Cloud Sync

Sign in with Google via Tasky ▼ → ☁️ Sign in to sync data across devices. Works fully offline — changes queue locally and sync when you're back online.

🛡️ Your data
Task Groups and Workspaces are preserved even after a data reset.
👥

Team Collaboration

Sign in, then go to Tasky ▼ → Create Collaboration. Share the 6-character code with teammates. As supervisor you get a Team column to assign tasks in real-time. Members see assigned tasks appear instantly.

👤 Quick assign
Type task text to::handle priority::high date::20may in the input for instant task assignment.
